# README imagery

[OpenPlayer cover](openplayer.webp) was created with the imagegen built-in image generator. The same tool created the original still for the short *Arctic Passage* demo clip.

## Generation prompts

**Cover**

> Create an ultra-wide 2.8:1 editorial cover for the open-source cinema player OpenPlayer. On the left, beautifully typeset the single word “OpenPlayer” in large ivory modern grotesk lettering, clear at phone width. On the right, a single physical 35 mm film ribbon twists into an open, forward-facing triangular loop, lit through amber photographic emulsion. Show precise sprocket holes, translucent frames and a faint teal reflection in dark optical glass. Rich charcoal background, deep shadows, restrained tungsten light, tactile macro studio photography. Balance type and sculpture across the middle of the wide frame, generous breathing room at the edges. No other words, badges, UI, logos or borders.

**Demo footage**

> An original cinematic film still in 16:9. Viewed from high above, a tiny warm-lit skiff leaves a delicate curving wake in the deep teal water of an Arctic inlet. One immense angular blue iceberg, translucent jade facets and a weathered white cap, fills the left half of the frame. The boat catches a shaft of late golden sunlight at center right. A few scattered flecks of ice give scale. Natural fine film grain, strong shapes, quiet human scale, richly textured editorial cinematography, crisp enough to read as a small media thumbnail. Full bleed, no typography, no graphics or watermarks.

## Player captures

[Playback](playback.webp) and [Media browser](media-browser.webp) are client-area captures of the Windows x64 release of OpenPlayer 2.0.0 with the language set to English. The original still was encoded as video, opened in the player and indexed by its media browser. The controls, paths, timestamps and thumbnails were drawn by OpenPlayer.
